Make fragmented giving, nonprofit discovery, and disconnected impact updates tangible before expanding into the bigger mission.

02 / Positioning

What visitors understand today

The current profile communicates a broad commitment to opportunity and helping people access resources. It says less about the specific problem you are addressing through Siren.

Current signal

“Building The Future Of Charitable Giving”

A mission-led founder identity, supported by a values-focused summary.

Stronger signal

Making charitable giving easier to discover, coordinate, and stay connected to.

This connects the mission to the donor experience Siren is building.

03 / Headline

Five stronger directions

Your current headline is: Founder @ Siren, Building The Future Of Charitable Giving. It establishes your role and ambition, but leaves the practical value of Siren open to interpretation.

Why refine it?

A donor, nonprofit leader, or potential partner arriving from a post should quickly understand what Siren is changing. The options below use the public product description and avoid unverified performance claims.

01

Founder of Siren | Making Charitable Giving More Personal, Social & Simple

Closest to the existing brand promise, with clearer language.

02

Building Siren | Discover Causes, Support Multiple Nonprofits & Track Your Giving in One Place

Most product-specific. Explains the core donor experience.

03

Founder @ Siren | Connecting Donors With Causes and Nonprofits That Matter

Human and relationship-led, suited to mission-driven conversations.

04

Building a More Connected Giving Experience | Founder, Siren

Concise and brand-oriented, with room for the mission to lead.

05

Founder of Siren | Helping Donors Discover Causes and Stay Connected to Their Impact

Emphasizes discovery and the ongoing relationship after a donation.

04 / About

Turn the belief into a clear story

The current About section expresses a sincere belief in opportunity and equitable access to resources. It is values-led, but does not name Siren’s specific user problem or explain how the platform approaches it.

Current About, excerpt

“I truly believe that the U.S. is the Land Of Opportunity, where hard work and perseverance can open doors that might otherwise seem closed…”

Strength: personal conviction. Gap: the connection to charitable giving and Siren is not explicit.

What the new version adds

The donor friction, the product’s role, the human belief behind it, and a clear invitation for donors and nonprofit teams to connect.

Suggested replacement About

I believe giving should feel as personal as the reason behind it. People care about many causes, but supporting them can mean navigating different websites, managing separate donations, and trying to keep track of the impact along the way. That friction can make a generous intention harder to act on. That is why I am building Siren. Siren is a modern giving platform designed to help people discover nonprofits, support multiple organizations in one place, and keep their giving connected. The bigger idea is simple: make generosity easier to act on, more social to share, and more meaningful to follow over time. I am especially interested in how technology can bring donors and nonprofits closer together without losing the human stories that make giving matter in the first place. If you are a donor who wants to explore causes, or a nonprofit working to reach people who care about your mission, I would love to connect and hear what a better giving experience should look like.

Before publishing, confirm the product details and preferred audience emphasis still match Siren’s current offering.
